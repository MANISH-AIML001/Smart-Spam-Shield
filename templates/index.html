How the model decides

Three layers work together on every scan — no single signal has to catch everything on its own.

TF‑IDF

Reads the words

Every word is weighted by how unusual and message-specific it is, so the model learns which word patterns tend to show up in spam.

Behaviour

Reads the structure

Nine extra signals — link count, capital-letter ratio, urgency words, phone numbers — catch spam that's been reworded to dodge word filters.

Ensemble

Three models vote

Logistic Regression, Random Forest and SVM each cast a weighted vote. Agreement across all three makes the verdict harder to fool.
